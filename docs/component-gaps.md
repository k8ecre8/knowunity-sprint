# Component gaps

Things built inline during a screen build because they are not in Storybook. One line each: what it is, where it was built. When a second screen needs the same thing, it gets promoted to `src/components/` and this line says so.

- `noteCard` (tones neutral, highlight, outlined, plain; leading icon or none; showTitle, showChevron): now a component, `src/components/NoteCard.tsx`, promoted when the Review summary needed the highlight tone for its come-back line. Used by Section summary (`SectionSummary.tsx`, outlined) and Review summary (`ReviewSummary.tsx`, highlight). `leading=badge` (a nested `planNode` done S, pass `badge` with its tone) was added when the Exam plan home needed the plan-complete card, `src/app/plan/PlanHome.tsx`.
- `topBar`: now a component, `src/components/TopBar.tsx` (Sep 2026), promoted from the two inline bars. `variant="home"` (menu, Pro / XP / streak counters, focus timer) on App home, `variant="plan"` (kebab only) on Exam plan home. Decoration throughout.
- `tabs` (Plan | Materials, two tabs filling the width, active with a Stroke/Heavy Border accent/blue/bold underline and text/primary, inactive with a Stroke/Border border/strong underline and text/disabled): Figma's `Tabs` is not editable and its label style is unbound, so it is built inline in Exam plan home with Body M Bold; the row is 32 tall to the frame's 29. Both tabs are inert because Materials is outside this flow.
- `sectionRow` (a section name in Body M Bold beside a Control/1400 book cell behind a border/strong divider, on background/input with a border/strong stroke at Radius/400): built inline in Exam plan home. Neither cell is a control here.
- `planPath` (the winding path: `planNode` M rows Space/200 above and below, alternately inset Space/1600 and Space/800, Body M Bold title over Body S Regular caption, the whole row the tap target when the node goes somewhere; and `phaseDivider`, a Body S Bold label between two border/strong hairlines): built inline in Exam plan home.
- `testDayPanel`: now a component, `src/components/TestDayPanel.tsx` (Sep 2026), promoted when the App home's exam-eve hero turned out to be a copy of the Exam plan home's complete-mode panel. Used by both (`size` S on plan home, M on app home).
- `confidenceReads` (before-plan and today ratings side by side, arrow between): the "How you felt" reads. Built inline in Review summary, `src/app/recall/[round]/summary/ReviewSummary.tsx`.
- `textBlock` (headline, optional caption): now a component, `src/components/TextBlock.tsx`, promoted when the Exam-eve repeat summary needed the headline with its caption shown. Used by Section summary (`SectionSummary.tsx`, caption hidden) and Exam-eve repeat summary (`EveSummary.tsx`, caption shown).
- `groupLabel` (label, trailing count): "Look at these before your test · 4 of 12". SPEC.md files it under `textBlock`, but a label with a trailing count is not a heading with a caption. Built inline in Exam-eve repeat summary, `src/app/recall/[round]/summary/EveSummary.tsx`.
- `summaryCard` expanded state: three named term slots cannot hold twelve rows, so a code-only `terms` prop was added to `src/components/SummaryCard.tsx` for the repeat summary's expanded and fully-open cards. Documented in its stories.
- `answerOption` (five positions on one bar, track background/surface, fill interactive/primary to the chosen position, chosen label in Headline M above): the confidence check's slider. Figma draws it as "slider (no component)", a 17-tall bar with no tap targets; built inline as five `role=radio` targets at Control/1200 over a Control/400 track in Confidence check, `src/app/recall/review/confidence/page.tsx`.
- `settingsHint` (a `noteCard` neutral with `microphone-off-01` telling the student how to re-enable the mic in Settings): no Figma frame or copy for the denied screen exists, so the note is composed from `NoteCard` in Mic denied, `src/app/recall/[round]/mic-denied/page.tsx`. Nothing built inline; listed so the copy gets a home when the frame is drawn.
- `bottomSheet` (headline, caption, showCaption, actions as children): now a component, `src/components/BottomSheet.tsx`, promoted when the Voice turn needed the same leave confirm the Typed turn had built inline. Used by Typed turn (`typed/page.tsx`) and Voice turn (`[term]/page.tsx`), both inside the scaffold's `bottomSheetOnly` with `showBottomSheetBackground`. Copy is Open 18, decided on the typed turn: "Leave this round?", Stay (Primary L), Leave (Text L). The section intro tray uses it too since Sep 2026, through `middleSection` and `showAppBar`.
- `voiceInput` (the push-to-talk control, Figma `recordingControl`): now a component, `src/components/VoiceInput.tsx`, promoted Sep 2026 when its design settled on one `accent/brand/bold` line at `Stroke/Bold` through every state (tokens `Stroke/Bold`, `motion.duration.spin` and `spin-slow` added for it). It carries the label above the ring, the idle escapes in an `idleActions` slot, the transcript card, send (`buttonIcon` Primary M), discard (`buttonIcon` Tertiary M `trash-01`) and retry. Used by Voice turn, `src/app/recall/[round]/[term]/page.tsx`, which maps its phases onto the component's states and passes the resting helper lines.
- `permissionAlert` (the mocked iOS mic prompt, Open 6; title, body, onDeny, onAllow): now a component, `src/components/PermissionAlert.tsx`, promoted when the Section intro tray needed the same prompt on Start that the Voice turn had built inline. Used by Section intro tray (`src/app/plan/[section]/intro/page.tsx`, on Start) and Voice turn (`[term]/page.tsx`, on the first control tap when a round is entered without the tray), both in `bottomSheetOnly` over the scaffold scrim. Its buttons keep iOS's own "Don't Allow" / "Allow", the one place the sentence-case rule is not applied, because the alert reproduces the OS.
- `introTray`: now `BottomSheet` with `showAppBar`, `onClose` and `middleSection` (Sep 2026), the mascot, heading and three rows in the middle section. Section intro tray, `src/app/plan/[section]/intro/page.tsx`. The screen keeps the scrim layer that closes it and holds it below the status bar.
- Transcript step (Open 3): now inside `voiceInput`. The ring's own line becomes the card holding the words under "Here’s what Knowie heard", with send and discard inside it, so there is no `noteCard` and no Send / Discard pair in `bottomContent`. Voice turn, `src/app/recall/[round]/[term]/page.tsx`.
- `bottomNav` (active: chat, plans, trophy, profile): now a component, `src/components/BottomNav.tsx`, promoted when the App home needed the same bar the Exam plan home had built inline, with `chat` active. Used by Exam plan home (`src/app/plan/PlanHome.tsx`, plans) and App home (`src/app/page.tsx`, chat). `hrefs` (Sep 2026) links Chat and Plans between the two homes; trophy and profile stay inert. Only `myai-chat` is still missing from `IconSlot`; its tab shows `send-01`, marked `data-placeholder-glyph`.
- `askKnowie` (the Ask Knowie bar: a round plus and a placeholder pill with a mic glyph): AI Chat is out of scope, so it is drawn and inert. The frame draws both at 52, which Control has no step for (`Control/1300` would be the name); here the pill hugs Space/300 padding around Body M Regular (48) and the plus takes the pill's height. Built inline in App home, `src/app/page.tsx`.
- `ai-quiz` glyph: not in `IconSlot`. App home's Quiz chip uses `file-question-02` as a placeholder, marked with `data-placeholder-glyph`. The other glyphs once listed here (`globe-01`, `target-04`, `book-open-02`, `clipboard-check`, `thumbs-up`, `lock-01`, `list`, `graduation-hat-02`, `upload-cloud-02`, `trophy-02`) were added to `IconSlot` Sep 2026 and the screens use them.
- App home middle-content scrim: the frames paint a linear gradient over the middle content behind the hero. There are no gradient tokens, so it is not drawn.
- `knowiePrompt` (mascotSlot over the responseBubble, pulled up by Space/negative-600 so the bubble's tail meets Knowie): the same block in Typed turn (`typed/page.module.css`) and Voice turn (`[term]/page.module.css`). Two uses, not yet a component.
- Summary `Card` wrapper (a `summaryCard` whose row count the screen decides: all rows when open, else three and an overflow row that expands it): the same function in Exam-eve repeat summary (`EveSummary.tsx`) and Review summary (`ReviewSummary.tsx`). Two uses, not yet a component.
- Full-width `Button`: now the `fullWidth` prop on `src/components/Button.tsx` (Sep 2026), replacing the `.actions > * > *` and `className` stretching on the three summaries, Mic denied, Confidence check, both turns and the intro tray.
- Headline S over Body M: now `textBlock` `size="S"` (Sep 2026). Used by App home's study reminder, Review summary's comparison and the test-day panel's headline.
